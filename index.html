FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is my data used?

SchoolNoter does not store, collect, or log any of your data. Your Student ID and password are sent directly to the official StudentVUE servers through a secure proxy — we never see or save your credentials. All grade data lives only in your browser session and is wiped when you log out or close the tab.

Is my password safe?

Yes. Your password is transmitted over HTTPS and is never stored on any server. The proxy forwards your credentials to MCPS StudentVUE and returns the response — it acts as a pass-through only. After login, a session token is used instead of your password.

What about the AI college checker?

The College Readiness Checker compares your profile against real Common Data Set (CDS) statistics that colleges publish annually. All calculations run locally in your browser — no data is sent to any server for this feature.

Can my school see that I use SchoolNoter?

SchoolNoter accesses the same StudentVUE system you'd normally use. Your school can see that a login occurred but cannot distinguish SchoolNoter from the official StudentVUE app or website.

Who built this?

Bunked is not affiliated with or endorsed by MCPS or Edupoint (the company behind StudentVUE).
